## 🔧 What I do
 
- **Firmware**: bare-metal, FreeRTOS and Zephyr RTOS applications in C and C++ across Nordic, NXP, STM32, ESP32 and Raspberry Pi microcontrollers. My work covers drivers, devicetree, peripherals, displays, BLE, CAN and secure boot.
- **Zephyr**: out-of-tree SoC and board ports and custom drivers. My first upstream contribution, merged into the Zephyr Project, is **[board support for the Waveshare Core2350B](https://github.com/zephyrproject-rtos/zephyr/pull/120314)**, an RP2350B board, covering both the Cortex-M33 and Hazard3 RISC-V clusters.
- **Hardware**: schematic capture and PCB layout in KiCad, from power architecture to board bring-up.
- **Teaching**: designing and delivering Zephyr RTOS courses at the *Laboratorio de Sistemas Embebidos* (UTN Facultad Regional Avellaneda) and mentoring student researchers on the lab's R&D projects.

## 🚀 Featured projects
 
| Project | What it is | Stack |
|---|---|---|
| [**frdm-mcxn236-sensor-hub**](https://github.com/carlassaraf/frdm-mcxn236-sensor-hub) | A CAN-aware environmental and motion monitor with an LVGL touchscreen dashboard. It boots through MCUboot with signed images and accepts firmware updates over SMP. | Zephyr · NXP MCX N · LVGL · CAN · MCUboot |
| [**tp_analyzer**](https://github.com/carlassaraf/tp_analyzer) | My final degree project: a low-cost, DIN-rail three-phase power-quality analyzer. It computes RMS, FFT and phase unbalance and runs motor current signature analysis (MCSA) for predictive maintenance. | Zephyr · RP2350 · DSP · KiCad |
| [**ble-sensor-node**](https://github.com/carlassaraf/ble-sensor-node) | An environmental sensor node on the nRF5340. It has a custom AHT10 Zephyr driver, encrypted BLE and an LVGL display, and is written in modern C++17 with a state-pattern FSM and no dynamic allocation. | Zephyr / NCS · nRF5340 · C++17 · BLE |
| [**zephyr_lpc43xx**](https://github.com/utn-fra-lse/zephyr_lpc43xx) | A work-in-progress out-of-tree Zephyr port for the NXP LPC4337 on the EDU-CIAA-NXP board. The GPIO, pinctrl and UART drivers work on hardware, and the repo includes a full porting guide. | Zephyr · LPC4337 · Devicetree |
 
## 🎓 Teaching & talks
 
Teaching is a big part of what I do. At the **Laboratorio de Sistemas Embebidos (LSE)** I work with colleagues on courses and open material:
 
- 🎤 **[Zephyr RTOS: one application, many boards](https://github.com/utn-fra-lse/cie2026-zephyr)**: a talk and technical paper presented at CIE 2026, with a live demo of a single app running on boards from six vendors. It covers devicetree, Kconfig and standardized drivers.
- 📚 **Zephyr RTOS Fundamentals**: course design, hands-on labs and automated assignment grading with GitHub Actions on `native_sim`.
- ⏱️ **[Intro to RTOS with FreeRTOS](https://github.com/utn-fra-lse/td2-rtos)**: two classes for *Técnicas Digitales II*. The same light-meter app on the LPC845 is written bare-metal first and then with FreeRTOS, so students see the difference on the board.
- 🧠 **[Dual-core MCU programming and debugging](https://github.com/carlassaraf/2024-jornadas-td2)**: a talk at the 6th LSE Conference (2024) with step-by-step examples on the RP2040 and RP2350, going from bare-metal on both cores to FreeRTOS + bare-metal with inter-core queues.
I also mentor the lab's student researchers and help coordinate its R&D projects.
